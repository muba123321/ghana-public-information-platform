Project Name: GHANA PUBLIC INFORMATION PLATFORM

# NEW CORE MODULE: GOVERNMENT PERFORMANCE AND HISTORICAL ACCOUNTABILITY

## 1. PURPOSE

Develop a comprehensive historical government performance tracking system within the Ghana Public Information Platform.

The purpose is to allow citizens to examine Ghana's development over time and understand what successive governments promised, what they achieved, what resources they used and what commitments remained unfinished.

Ghana has successive electoral and government administrations. The platform must preserve a continuous historical record across these transitions.

The historical module must cover all relevant ministries, public institutions, sectors, programmes and national development indicators.

## 2. GOVERNMENT ADMINISTRATION HISTORY

Create a historical directory of Ghanaian government administrations.

For each administration, maintain:

- Administration identifier.
- Political party in government.
- Start date.
- End date.
- President.
- Government manifesto.
- Published national development objectives.
- Major policy programmes.
- Ministry structures.
- Historical budget information.
- Reported achievements.
- Independent national outcomes.
- Outstanding commitments.
- Original supporting documents.

Preserve historical records even when ministries are renamed, reorganized or replaced.

Use actual historical dates and verified institutional information.

## 3. MANIFESTO AND POLICY COMMITMENT TRACKING

Where public documents are available, extract and organize commitments from:

- Political party manifestos.
- Government policy statements.
- National development plans.
- Presidential announcements.
- Ministry strategic plans.
- Budget statements.
- Official programme documents.

For every commitment, record:

- Original wording.
- Original source.
- Responsible institution.
- Announcement date.
- Target.
- Target deadline.
- Relevant sector.
- Budget allocation, if available.
- Implementation progress.
- Reported outcome.
- Independent verification.
- Final status.

Distinguish campaign promises from formal government policies, approved programmes and funded projects.

Do not treat every manifesto statement as an approved government commitment.

## 4. GOVERNMENT PERFORMANCE COMPARISON

Create a dedicated interface where citizens can compare successive administrations.

Comparison categories should include:

### Economic performance
- GDP growth.
- Inflation.
- Public debt.
- Government revenue.
- Government expenditure.
- Budget deficit.
- Debt servicing.
- Exchange rates.
- Unemployment.
- Poverty.

### Agriculture
- Crop production.
- Food security.
- Food imports.
- Agricultural exports.
- Farmer support.
- Agricultural employment.

### Education
- Teacher recruitment.
- Teacher distribution.
- School infrastructure.
- Student enrolment.
- Educational expenditure.
- Examination outcomes.

### Healthcare
- Hospital construction.
- Healthcare infrastructure.
- Medical personnel.
- Healthcare expenditure.
- Access to healthcare.
- Maternal and child health outcomes.

### Infrastructure
- Roads constructed.
- Roads rehabilitated.
- Bridges.
- Housing.
- Water infrastructure.
- Electricity access.
- Public transportation.

### Social development
- Employment.
- Poverty reduction.
- Social protection.
- Regional development.
- Access to public services.

The platform must use consistent indicator definitions, measurement units, geographical coverage and reporting periods wherever possible.

## 5. HISTORICAL PERFORMANCE VISUALIZATION

Build interactive visualizations that allow users to:

- Select an administration.
- Compare two or more administrations.
- Select a specific ministry.
- Select a sector.
- Examine national indicators over time.
- View annual performance.
- Compare targets with actual results.
- Examine inherited commitments.
- Examine completed and unfinished programmes.

Display government administration periods on relevant historical charts.

Provide access to the original data and documents behind every comparison.

## 6. PROMISES AND COMMITMENTS ACROSS GOVERNMENT TRANSITIONS

This is a particularly important requirement.

A government commitment must not disappear when a new administration takes office.

If an administration starts a project and a subsequent administration inherits it, maintain its entire history.

For every continuing commitment, record:

- Original administration.
- Original announcement.
- Original target.
- Original budget.
- Progress at the end of the original administration.
- Outstanding work inherited by the next administration.
- Additional funding or revisions.
- Progress under subsequent administrations.
- Final delivery status.

Do not automatically credit an entire project to the administration that completes it. Preserve the contributions and timeline of each administration.

## 7. HISTORICAL ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL INDICATORS

Create a national historical database that can store annual, quarterly and monthly indicators.

Where available, include historical information dating back multiple decades.

Support:

- Long-term trends.
- Administration-specific timelines.
- Year-over-year changes.
- Baseline comparisons.
- Target comparisons.
- Historical revisions.
- Data-source comparisons.

Allow users to see how Ghana's economic and social indicators evolved over time.

## 8. PERFORMANCE EVIDENCE AND TRANSPARENCY

The platform should not generate arbitrary political ratings or declare an administration successful or unsuccessful.

Instead, create transparent, measurable performance profiles.

For every administration, present:

- Published commitments.
- Targets established.
- Resources allocated.
- Funds spent.
- Reported achievements.
- Independently published outcomes.
- Unfinished commitments.
- Unresolved questions.
- Data availability and reliability.

Every indicator must have a source and reporting period.

Where information is missing, clearly display that information is unavailable.

Do not penalize an administration automatically because information has not been published.

## 9. FAIR HISTORICAL COMPARISON

Historical comparisons must account for:

- Economic conditions inherited from previous administrations.
- International economic shocks.
- Global commodity prices.
- Changes in statistical methodologies.
- Changes in institutional responsibilities.
- Population growth.
- Project implementation timelines.
- Delayed policy effects.
- Changes in geographical coverage.
- Historical data revisions.

Separate observed changes from causal claims about government policies.

Do not attribute every improvement or deterioration in a national indicator to the administration in office.

## 10. USER EXPERIENCE

Create a dedicated section called:

GOVERNMENT PERFORMANCE & HISTORY

It should contain:

1. Historical administration directory.
2. Administration profiles.
3. Manifesto and commitment tracker.
4. Historical performance indicators.
5. Administration comparison interface.
6. Ministry performance history.
7. Outstanding commitments.
8. Economic and social development timelines.
9. Original sources and supporting documents.

The interface should be visually engaging, easy to navigate and accessible to ordinary Ghanaian citizens.

## 11. IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITY

Do not attempt to populate decades of historical information immediately.

First, establish the historical database architecture.

Then:

1. Create the administration entity and historical timeline.
2. Connect administrations to ministries and institutions.
3. Connect commitments to their originating administrations.
4. Connect indicators to annual and quarterly results.
5. Build historical charts.
6. Build the administration comparison interface.
7. Populate a small set of historical indicators using verified public sources.

This module must be designed as an integral component of the Ghana Public Information Platform, not a separate application.
